# **App Name**: DemoTrade AI

## Core Features:

- Demo Account: Simulate a demo trading environment with $10,000 starting balance.
- Robot Selection: Allow users to select one of three AI trading robots with distinct strategies. The robots trade using a simple market simulator 'tool'.
- Real-time Monitoring: Display real-time trading results and the user's current balance.
- Trade History: Provide a detailed log of all executed trades with relevant information.
- Market Simulator: Simulate trades, calculate profit/loss, and update the demo account balance accordingly.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep sky blue (#4169E1) to evoke a sense of trust, security, and forward-thinking technology.
- Background color: Light gray (#D3D3D3) to provide a neutral backdrop that doesn't distract from the trading data.
- Accent color: Bright green (#00FF00) for positive balance changes and successful trades, signaling gains and progress.
- Font pairing: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for headlines and 'Inter' (sans-serif) for body text to maintain a modern and easily readable interface.
- Use simple, crisp icons for robot selection, trade history, and account information. Icons should be monochromatic, using the primary blue color.
- Clean and structured layout with clear sections for balance display, robot controls, and trade history. Use consistent spacing and alignment.
- Subtle animations to indicate trade executions and balance updates. For example, a brief highlight on a successful trade or a smooth transition in the balance display.
